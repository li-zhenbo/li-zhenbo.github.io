\section{Motivation and Advances over the 2016 GPLP Method}

The generalized Pad\'e--Lindstedt--Poincar\'e (GPLP) method was established in the authors' prior work (Nonlinear Dynamics, 2016). That foundational paper demonstrated GPLP on oscillators with cubic (three-term) and quintic symmetric polynomial potentials. Three limitations motivated the present work:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item The 2016 paper only handled symmetric potentials ($c_1x + c_3x^3 + c_5x^5$). Realistic oscillators often have \textbf{full polynomial restoring forces}:
\begin{equation}
g(x) = c_1x + c_2x^2 + c_3x^3 + c_4x^4 + c_5x^5
\label{eq:full_quintic}
\end{equation}
\item The heteroclinic approximant in 2016 was less efficient; new, more accurate approximant types are needed.
\item The 2016 paper did not address oscillators with \textbf{rational restoring forces}.
\end{enumerate}

This paper extends GPLP to: (1)~the \textbf{entire $\Phi^6$-Van der Pol oscillator} with full quintic polynomial including $x^2$ and $x^4$ terms; and (2)~the \textbf{generalized Duffing-Harmonic-Van der Pol oscillator}:
\begin{equation}
\ddot{x} + \frac{\lambda x + \delta x^3}{1 + \nu x^2} = \varepsilon(\mu_c + \mu_1x + \mu_2x^2)\dot{x}
\label{eq:dhvdp}
\end{equation}

\section{System Models}

\subsection{The Full $\Phi^6$-Van der Pol Oscillator}

\begin{equation}
\ddot{x} + c_1x + c_2x^2 + c_3x^3 + c_4x^4 + c_5x^5 = \varepsilon(\mu_c + \mu_1x + \mu_2x^2)\dot{x}
\label{eq:phi6full}
\end{equation}

The full polynomial ($c_2\neq0$, $c_4\neq0$) creates \textbf{asymmetric multi-well potentials}: wells of different depths separated by a saddle at different heights, where homoclinic and heteroclinic orbits coexist with qualitatively different geometries.

\section{New Generalized Pad\'e Approximant Types}

The key innovation is new approximant forms with an \textbf{additional free frequency parameter $\omega$}:

\subsection{Improved Homoclinic Approximant}

The 2016 method implicitly fixed $\omega=1$:
\begin{equation}
\text{GPA}[L/M]x(t) = \frac{\sum_{i=0}^{L}\alpha_i\,\text{Sech}^i(\boldsymbol{\omega} t)}{1+\sum_{i=1}^{M}\beta_i\,\text{Sech}^i(\boldsymbol{\omega} t)}
\label{eq:homoclinic_improved}
\end{equation}
where $\omega$ is now a free parameter, solved simultaneously with $\alpha_i$, $\beta_i$.

\subsection{Heteroclinic Approximants (Two New Types)}

\textbf{Type A}---rational Tanh with free $\omega$:
\begin{equation}
\text{GPA}[L/M]x(t) = \frac{\sum_{i=0}^{L}\alpha_i\,\text{Tanh}^i(\omega t)}{1+\sum_{i=1}^{M}\beta_i\,\text{Tanh}^i(\omega t)}
\label{eq:hetero_a}
\end{equation}

\textbf{Type B}---combined Sech-Tanh, essential when both orbit types coexist at the same saddle:
\begin{equation}
\text{GPA}[L,M]x(t) = \frac{\sum_{i=0}^{L_1}\alpha_i\,\text{Sech}^i(\omega t) + \sum_{j=0}^{L_2}\gamma_j\,\text{Tanh}^j(\omega t)}{1+\sum_{i=1}^{M_1}\beta_i\,\text{Sech}^i(\omega t) + \sum_{j=1}^{M_2}\delta_j\,\text{Tanh}^j(\omega t)}
\label{eq:hetero_b}
\end{equation}

Type~B resolves the fundamental conflict in asymmetric potentials: the higher saddle's homoclinic orbit has a Sech-like symmetric approach, while the heteroclinic orbit connecting unequal saddles has a Tanh-like asymmetric character. A pure Tanh (Type~A) cannot simultaneously capture both; the combined Sech+Tanh (Type~B) can.

\section{GPLP Procedure (Refined)}

The perturbation procedure follows four steps, as in the 2016 paper, but with the new approximant types and $\omega$:

\begin{description}[leftmargin=2em]
\item[Step I] Zero-order $x_0$: expand power series $\to$ GPA with $\omega$ as unknown. Solve simultaneously for $\alpha_i$, $\beta_i$, $\omega$ via coefficient matching + boundary condition.
\item[Step II] $x_1$ and $\mu_{c0}$: Tanh-prefixed GPA; $\eta_{L_1}(\mu_{c0})=0$ gives the zero-order bifurcation parameter.
\item[Step III] $x_2$: polynomial-based GPA; $\lambda_{L_2}=0$ determines $d_0$ or $d_1$.
\item[Step IV] $x_3$ and $\mu_{c2}$: $\sigma_{L_3}(\mu_{c2})=0$ determines second-order correction.
\end{description}

The final solution and bifurcation parameter:
\begin{equation}
\boxed{x(t)=\sum_{i=0}^{3}\varepsilon^i\,\text{GPA}[L_i/M_i]x_i(t)+O(\varepsilon^4)}
\label{eq:final_sol}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\mu_c=\mu_{c0}+\varepsilon^2\mu_{c2}+O(\varepsilon^3)}
\label{eq:final_mu}
\end{equation}

\section{Results: $\Phi^6$-VDP Oscillator}

\subsection{Conservative Limit}

For oscillator (37) with $c_1=-1$, $c_2=0$, $c_3=1$, $c_4=0$, $c_5=-0.1$, the phase portrait (Fig.\ 1) reveals coexisting homoclinic and heteroclinic orbits at saddle $H_2$. The $\omega$-parameterized Sech approximant achieves $<1\%$ deviation from Runge--Kutta.

\subsection{Asymmetric Potential, Moderate Damping}

With $c_2\neq0$ and/or $c_4\neq0$:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{Both homoclinic orbits} (left and right wells): $<1\%$ error at $\varepsilon=1$.
\item \textbf{Heteroclinic orbits} connecting unequal saddles: Type~B essential; Type~A shows 5--8\% error vs.\ $<1.5\%$ for Type~B.
\item \textbf{Critical bifurcation parameters} predicted analytically across a wide parameter range (Tables~2--8).
\end{itemize}

\section{Results: Duffing-Harmonic-VDP Oscillator}

For the rational restoring force oscillator (\ref{eq:dhvdp}), validated at both small and large $\varepsilon$:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item Homoclinic orbits at $\varepsilon=0.5$, 1: error $<1.5\%$
\item Heteroclinic orbits at $\varepsilon=0.5$, 1, 2: error $<2\%$
\item GPLP handles the rational $g(x)$ without procedural modification.
\end{itemize}

\section{Accuracy: 2022 vs.\ 2016 GPLP}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{Symmetric + small $\varepsilon$:} both versions similar.
\item \textbf{Asymmetric, $\varepsilon=1$:} $\omega$-parameterized approximants reduce homoclinic error by 40--60\%.
\item \textbf{Heteroclinic, asymmetric:} Type~B (2022) achieves $<1.5\%$ vs.\ 5--8\% for 2016 Tanh-only form.
\item \textbf{Rational $g(x)$:} improvement more pronounced due to richer potential landscapes.
\end{itemize}

\section{Why Type~B Matters}

The geometric insight behind Type~B:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item The higher saddle's homoclinic orbit has \textbf{Sech-like} symmetric approach.
\item The heteroclinic orbit connecting unequal saddles has \textbf{Tanh-like} asymmetric character.
\item A pure Tanh (Type~A) cannot simultaneously capture the homoclinic orbit's symmetry.
\item The combined Sech+Tanh (Type~B) resolves this by leveraging both basis functions.
\end{itemize}

\section{Key Findings}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{Full $\Phi^6$ coverage:} GPLP extended from symmetric cubic/quintic to the full quintic polynomial ($c_1$--$c_5$), enabling asymmetric multi-well potential analysis.
\item \textbf{Two new heteroclinic approximants:} Type~A (rational Tanh) for pure heteroclinic; Type~B (combined Sech-Tanh) for coexisting H/H orbits, giving 40--80\% accuracy improvement for asymmetric cases.
\item \textbf{$\omega$-parameterized homoclinic approximant:} Free frequency parameter adapts to different potential shapes.
\item \textbf{Rational restoring force:} DH-VDP oscillator solved without GPLP framework modification.
\item \textbf{Systematic multi-parameter studies:} 8 parameter sets each for homoclinic and heteroclinic bifurcations.
\item \textbf{Completes the GPLP framework} for polynomial and rational restoring forces.
\end{enumerate}
