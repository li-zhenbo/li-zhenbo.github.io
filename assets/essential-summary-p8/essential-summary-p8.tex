\section{Background and Motivation}

Classical Pade\'{e} approximation has been widely applied in nonlinear dynamics. Various researchers extended it by replacing polynomial components with exponential functions to better capture asymptotic homoclinic decay. However, these modifications were proposed case-by-case without a \textbf{unifying theoretical framework}.

This paper (the English version of the GPA method) proposes an intrinsic extension: numerator and denominator are generalized from polynomials to \textbf{series of any function type}, unifying all prior modifications. It further extends the scope to \textbf{both homoclinic and heteroclinic orbits} across three oscillator classes.

\section{Core Innovation}

\subsection{Generalized Definition}

Let $\widehat{H}_m = \{P: P(z)=\sum_{i=0}^m a_i g_i(z)^i\}$ extend $H_m$. The \textbf{GPA}[$L/M$]$_f$ is $P_L/Q_M\in G(L,M)$ satisfying $f(z) - P_L/Q_M = \mathcal{O}(z^{L+M+1})$. Classical Pade\'{e} ($g_i(z)=z^i$) and all quasi-Pade\'{e} variants become special cases.

\subsection{Two Novel Hyperbolic-Function Approximants}

For $\ddot{x} + g(x) = 0$ with saddle $H(h,0)$:

\textbf{Homoclinic} ($x(\pm\infty)=h$):
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\text{GPA}_{\text{hom}}[L/M] = \frac{\displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{L} \alpha_i \operatorname{sech}^i t}{\displaystyle 1 + \sum_{i=1}^{M} \beta_i \operatorname{sech}^i t}}.
\end{equation}

\textbf{Heteroclinic} ($x(\pm\infty)=h_{1,2}$):
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\text{GPA}_{\text{het}}[L/M] = \frac{\displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{L} \alpha_i \tanh^i t}{\displaystyle 1 + \sum_{i=1}^{M} \beta_i \tanh^i t}}.
\end{equation}

Coefficients $\{\alpha_i,\beta_i\}$ are found by Taylor-expanding at $t=0$ and matching with the power series $\{a_i\}$ from substituting $x(t)=\sum a_i t^i$ into $\ddot{x}+g(x)=0$, with the initial amplitude from $\int_h^a g(x)dx = 0$. The hyperbolic basis functions automatically satisfy the asymptotic boundary conditions.

\section{Key Results}

\subsection{Cubic--Quintic Duffing Equation}
\begin{equation}
\ddot{x} + c_1 x + c_3 x^3 + c_5 x^5 = 0,\qquad c_1=2,\; c_3=-10,\; c_5=9.
\end{equation}
Five fixed points: three centers, two saddles. Two homoclinic orbits and one heteroclinic pair coexist. GPA[$4/4$] and GPA[$7/7$] capture all orbits.

\subsection{Duffing Equation with $\Phi^6$ Potential}
\begin{equation}
\ddot{x} + c_1 x + c_2 x^2 + c_3 x^3 + c_4 x^4 + c_5 x^5 = 0.
\end{equation}
With $c_1=c_3=c_5=1, c_2=c_4=-1.5$, three saddle points allow both homoclinic and heteroclinic orbits. GPA[$5/5$] and GPA[$7/7$] outperform the classical Pade\'{e} approximant at the same order.

\begin{figure}[htbp]
\centering

\caption{Homoclinic and heteroclinic orbits of the $\Phi^6$ oscillator (Original Fig.\ 3). Solid: Runge--Kutta. Dotted: GPA. Dashed: classical Pade\'{e} approximant.}
\end{figure}

\subsection{Generalized Duffing--Harmonic Oscillator}
\begin{equation}
\ddot{x} + \frac{\lambda x + \mu x^3}{1 + \nu x^2} = 0.
\end{equation}
With $\lambda=-1, \mu=5, \nu=5$: GPA[$3/3$] captures two homoclinic orbits. With $\lambda=3, \mu=-5, \nu=2$: GPA[$8/8$] captures heteroclinic orbits.

\section{Significance}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{Unifying framework:} GPA subsumes classical Pade\'{e} and all quasi-Pade\'{e} variants as special cases.
\item \textbf{Dual homoclinic--heteroclinic pipeline:} $\operatorname{sech}^i t$ / $\tanh^i t$ dual approximant construction handles both orbit types in one framework.
\item \textbf{Superior accuracy:} Hyperbolic-function GPA achieves demonstrably higher accuracy than polynomial-based Pade\'{e} at the same order, especially for strongly nonlinear cases.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Limitations}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item Conservative systems only ($\varepsilon=0$).
\item Approximation order empirical; no systematic error bound.
\item High-order coefficient matching may become ill-conditioned.
\end{itemize}

\section{Connection to the GHFP/MGHFP Series}

The GPA method and the GHFP/MGHFP family share the mathematical toolkit of rational function approximation but pursue complementary routes: GPA constructs homoclinic/heteroclinic orbits via hyperbolic-function approximants in the time domain; GHFP/MGHFP uses angular-domain perturbation for amplitude--parameter relationships. The $\operatorname{sech}^i t$ basis introduced here also appears in the GHFP homoclinic construction $x = a\sin^2\varphi + b$.
