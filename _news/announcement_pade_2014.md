The paper *A Generalized Padé Approximation Method of Solving Homoclinic and Heteroclinic Orbits of Strongly Nonlinear Autonomous Oscillators* is published in **Chinese Physics B** (Vol. 23, No. 12, 120501, 2014).

This English paper extends the **Generalized Padé Approximation (GPA)** method first proposed in Chinese in 力学学报 (2013). It introduces two novel hyperbolic-function approximants — the sech-based form for homoclinic orbits and the tanh-based form for heteroclinic orbits — and validates them on three oscillator classes: cubic–quintic Duffing, Φ⁶ potential, and Duffing-harmonic oscillators. All solutions match Runge–Kutta numerical results with high accuracy.
